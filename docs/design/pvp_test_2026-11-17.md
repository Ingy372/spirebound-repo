# Bot-vs-bot PvP spell test, 2026-11-17 (task J15)

Jay: "you set up the play test between two bots, have them test everything you need." Two level 300 test characters
online at once (`python tools/pvp_routes.py --run`, runner `tools/pvp_duel.py`), one standing still as the target, one
casting. The server trace (`/pilot trace on`) logs every cast: damage to the player, whether it was held (rooted or
slowed) 0.4 s later, and whether it moved. Targets heal now and then, so a few damage numbers are negative; wild
boars near the test spot add a little damage too.

| Scenario | What should happen | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Arcanist casts every attack spell at a player | about half a full hit; nothing moves or holds the player | **Pass.** Ember Strike: full 173-250, player took 110; Storm Lash full 260-370, took 143. Gravity Well and Repulse did not move the player. |
| Ranger | half damage; Snare and Pinning Volley hold; Recoil Shot and Scatterwind don't move a player | **Pass.** Both holds landed; no knock-back on the player. |
| Mystic | half damage; Rimebind holds; Overgrowth right after does not (4 s immunity); Tidecrash doesn't push | **Pass.** |
| Vanguard | Iron Reach pulls a player; Faultline and Mountainbreaker hold; Shield Bash right after a hold doesn't hold again | **Pass.** Pulled 1 tile (and 3 tiles in the first duel, where Shield Bash also knocked the player back a tile). |
| Haste against roots | a target that casts Quickstep the moment it is held is freed | **Pass**, 4 of 4: "this freed you from a hold". |
| Undying Oath against a player's killing blow | survives on 1 health | **Pass.** 60 health, a ~110 hit took exactly 59. |
| Lifeseed against a player's killing blow | does NOT save (monsters only) | **Pass.** The Mystic died; the kill was logged as unjustified. |
| Wild PvP arena event | full holds, no immunity, pushes move players | **Pass after a fix** (below): Tidecrash pushed the player 2 tiles, Bramble Patch held them. |
| Barrier rune | the Arcanist makes and uses one; others can't | **Pass.** Arcanist placed a barrier; the build restricts the rune to Arcanists. |
| Secure mode | area spells don't hit clean players while secure mode is on | **Pass** (Tibia's own rule): the first Glacier Core skipped the player until the bot turned secure mode off. |

**Bug found and fixed:** the arena scored every event except Last Man Standing as a team game. Wild PvP has no teams,
so it ended as a draw one second after it began. Every free-for-all now plays like Last Man Standing (test added).

**Not a bug:** a few "You are exhausted" messages came from spell cooldowns left over from earlier test sessions
(cooldowns are kept when you log out); the same spells in a fresh run cast fine, against monsters and players.
