# Grok's market research (summary), 2026-11-25

Source: Jay's private Grok page "Spirebound — MMORPG Systems & Market Intelligence Database" (sessions of 21 and 25 Nov 2026). Summarised by Claude; numbers are Grok's, with its labels (VERIFIED = 2+ sources, SINGLE = one source).

## Findings
- Most servers' "online" counts are padded by multi-clients, bots and trainers. Honest ones: Miracle 7.4 (460 of 466 unique), RubinOT Elysian (1,198 of 1,387).
- Launch spikes collapse: Exordion Bravora 3,129 → 226; Kaldrox avg 2,402 → about 94; Realera 3,079 → 36. Seasonal launches hold for 3–4 weeks (Shadow-Illusion owner).
- No-wipe servers with strict server-side anti-cheat last: Tibiantis 6+ years, a few hundred real players.
- Many systems without an identity fails: Halera, 50+ systems and daily patches, about 5 online.
- No SAO-themed Tibia server has measurable players (Frontier Online 0–1). Sell the systems, not the theme.
- Player sentiment: hate visible pay-to-win, padded counts, alt-character PK, wipes without carryover; time-poor adults want short sessions and catch-up.

## Other systems worth a later look (Grok's "adopt" list)
Catch-up XP keyed to the top level (late joiners), random stat lamps as exploration rewards, shareable build codes, fusing 3 duplicate drops into an upgrade material (a sink), a permadeath memorial wall for the Death Game, a season-end condition tied to the last boss, unannounced per-floor rifts. Avoid: paid upgrade odds, pay-for-power VIP, uncapped stat scrolls, paid AFK training, staff-paid guild stipends, donor-fired server boosts, online-scaled respawn.
