# What top OT servers have in common (research, 2026-11-08)

Snapshot of otservlist.org taken Sunday 2026-11-08 around 19:20 UTC (read through a browser; player counts are a single moment). "Unique" = distinct people, so a big gap between players and unique players means lots of multi-client/bot characters.

## The five servers looked at
| Server | Type | Online (unique) | Stand-out features |
|---|---|---|---|
| **RubinOT** (rubinot.com.br) | Brazil, Canary-style global 15.x, 80x, 17 worlds | ~12,250 total (Elysian world 1,120 / 974 unique) | Own client (autoloot, loot highlight, bosstiary tracker, managed loot containers), battle-pass seasons, "Ascension" seasons, linked tasks, weekly Castle event, Twitch "Live Store" rewards, one account for all worlds, supporter packs |
| **Aurera Global** | Brazil, 15.0, 200x, never reset | 1,484 (643) | Global quests, client that "helps with in-game actions" (automation) |
| **Cyleria** (cyleria.pl) | Poland, 8.60 custom, 300x | 654–812 (301) | **Built-in bot with ready-made hunting routes**, player-run shops, 3x XP every weekend, **Tower Siege** (PvP event on equal terms regardless of level), boss timers on the website, daily points, "completely free" (best items only from play), wiki, big Discord |
| **Baiak-Ilusion** | Brazil, 8.6 PvP, 400x | 659 (308) | Autoloot, offline trainer, cast system, warzones, city war, weapon upgrades, party XP bonus |
| **Gunzodus** (gunzodus.net) | Europe, 150x, 10.x and 15.30 clients | 441 (232) | New hardcore world with a 30-day launch championship, 0% death loss until level 100, faster early skills, Castle Wars, automated PvP events, rotating weekend bonus, character bazaar, cast |

## Features players expect (most top servers have them)
- Own custom client, often with an **Android** version
- **Autoloot** and loot highlighting
- **Scheduled automatic events** (PvP events, boss spawns, holiday events)
- **Weekend / rotating bonuses** (double XP, rapid respawn, double loot)
- **Guild territory war** (castle, tower, city war)
- **Offline or static training**
- **Cast / livestream** support (watch other players)
- Task / bestiary / bosstiary tracking, boss timers
- Coin shop with cosmetics and convenience
- Wiki + Discord community

## Why players quit (from OTLand threads, 2025–2026)
1. Pay-to-win.
2. Short server life / fear of resets (so a reset must feel like a fresh launch, not a loss).
3. Bad first 2 hours (bugs, early deaths, confusing start).
4. Early XP too strong, then nothing to do mid-game.
5. Real-money traders and launch rushers who sell out after a month.
6. Huge level gaps with no catch-up.
7. Plain x1 real-map clones.
8. Account theft and shop/bazaar bugs.
9. Multi-client/bot inflation (visible on server lists).
