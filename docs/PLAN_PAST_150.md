# Things to earn past level 150 (plan, roadmap R12, 2026-11-13)

Written for Jay. Nothing here is built yet; it is the plan for when Floors 5-10 get the same care Floors 1-2 got. Each idea gives a reason to keep playing that is not just a bigger number.

1. **The gear ladder keeps going.** The table in `tools/gen_gear.py` already holds three pieces per vocation per floor (a rare drop, a quest piece, the boss piece). Floors 3-10 get their rows the same way, so every floor boss is worth farming for its best piece. From Floor 5 the boss pieces gain a small set bonus when two from neighbouring floors are worn together.
2. **Lost Arts.** The rare spells from quests, secrets and bosses (DESIGN 5.3b) become the main unlock past 150: one per floor from 5 to 10, each from a different source (a secret, a fishing legend, a private boss kill, a Hardcore-only quest...).
3. **Mastery titles per floor.** Kill every monster kind on a floor 500 times, find all its secrets and catch all its fish: a floor master title and an outfit colour. Account-wide, so they carry across seasons.
4. **The Underkeep's deep wings** (Wings 4-6) as the long-term group goal: a weekly key, a boss per wing, cosmetic rewards and Spire Scrap.
5. **Twin Fang** stays the rare unique skill for the very top (Floor 8 trial).
6. **Season Path** keeps feeding cosmetics and house items for everyone, so a player who stops climbing still has goals.

Order (my recommendation): the gear table for Floors 3-5 first (cheap, the generator exists), then Lost Arts, then mastery titles. All of it waits until the friends test shows Floors 1-2 are fun (R13).
