# Spirebound rules and the punishment ladder

Written from `tools/player_rules.py` (`python tools/player_rules.py --write-md`); edit that file, not this one. The same rules are on the website and the sign-up page. Claude's draft, Jay can change any of it.

## In short (what `!rules` shows in game)
- Be decent in public chat: no slurs, no threats, no spam. The chat filter blocks the worst words in English, Portuguese and Spanish.
- Player killing is part of the game where the zones allow it; hunting newcomers again and again is not, and the skull and bounty rules punish it.
- Only the cavebot built into the Spirebound client is allowed. Third-party bots and macros are cheating.
- Bugs: report them with !report, never use them for gain.
- No selling characters, items or gold for real money.
- Report a player: say !report <name>, <what happened>. A gamemaster reads every report.

## What is not allowed

### In chat and towards other players
- Slurs, hate speech, threats, or picking on someone for who they are (where they come from, their religion, gender, sexuality, a disability).
- Sexual messages or harassment of any kind.
- Following or harassing someone outside the game, or sharing anyone's personal details (real name, address, photos, accounts elsewhere).
- Spam: flooding a channel, the same message over and over, or advertising other servers, websites or anything for sale.
- Pretending to be a gamemaster, a member of staff or one of the tower's NPCs.

### Names
- Rude names, names from other games or books, names of real, well-known people, and names that look like a staff title (GM, Admin, Tutor...). The sign-up page refuses most of them; a gamemaster renames the rest.

### Cheating
- Any bot, macro, auto-clicker or program other than the Spirebound client, or a changed copy of our client.
- Hiding the built-in cavebot, or getting around its cost or its limits in fights with players.
- Auto-fishing.
- Using a bug, a hole in the map or a broken quest for gain. Found one? Say !report and tell us; we thank people who do.
- Trying to get into someone else's account, or attacking or overloading the server.
- Running more than 3 game clients on one computer at the same time.

### Money and trades
- Selling or buying characters, accounts, items or gold for real money, or offering to.
- Scams: lying about what a trade is, swapping items at the last second, fake promises of payment. Trades are at your own risk, but a deliberate scam is punished. Staff can see the market and trade logs.

### Fights
- Hunting the same newer player again and again. Player killing is part of the game where the zones allow it; bullying is not, and the skull and bounty rules punish it.
- Asking a friend to get killed, or killing your own characters, to farm bounties, rewards or leaderboard places.

## What is allowed
- The cavebot and helpers built into the Spirebound client. While the cavebot runs you earn 45% of the experience and half the loot, and the cut lasts 10 minutes after you switch it off. Auto-targeting on its own costs nothing.
- Extra characters, and playing several accounts at once (up to 3 clients on one computer). Extra characters may hunt anywhere, not only in town.
- Training while online.
- Player killing where the zone allows it, inside the skull, bounty and protection rules (see PvP rules).

## Bots and helpers
- Our own client is the only allowed client; its helpers are built in so nobody needs anything else.
- Secrets, rare chests and the Twin Fang trial never trigger while the cavebot is on. Boss rooms do not let a cavebot character in.
- In fights with players the helpers only heal: no automatic haste, attacks or counters, and the cavebot stops walking.
- Suspicious patterns are logged for staff to look at. Nobody is banned automatically.
- Third-party bots or a hidden cavebot: first time, a 7-day ban and the gains removed; second time, a permanent ban.

## Never pay-to-win
- The donation shop sells looks, house decorations and conveniences only. Nothing in it gives damage, defence, healing, experience, skill speed, loot or gear stats. No experience boosts, no loot boxes, and shop items can never be gambled.
- Shop items can be traded on the market for gold, so players can earn them by playing.

## Fair play for the Death Game
- Only monsters, bosses, traps and the tower itself can retire a Death Game character. A death where another player did real damage in the last 60 seconds counts as a player death instead (see Difficulty).
- A server crash or server lag that kills a Death Game character is undone by a gamemaster. Ask within a day.

## Bans are public
- Every ban for cheating or real-money trading is listed with its reason in the Exile Ledger on this site.
- A ban covers the person's other accounts too.

## What happens to rule breakers
1. A warning from a gamemaster.
2. A mute: 1 hour, then 24 hours, then 7 days.
3. A ban of the character: 1 day, then 7 days.
4. A ban of the account: 30 days.
5. A permanent ban. Cheating with third-party bots, selling accounts or gold for money, or abusing a bug for gain skips straight to step 3 or 5.

## For gamemasters
- `/reports` lists open reports (`/reports clear` when done). `/mute <name>, <minutes>` and `/unmute <name>`.
- `/gmlog` shows the last GM actions. Every mute, restore, calendar and scope change is written there and in the server log.
- Unfair Death Game deaths (a server crash or server lag): `/restore <name>`.
