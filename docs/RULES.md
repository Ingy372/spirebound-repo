# Spirebound rules and the punishment ladder (roadmap R14, 2026-11-13)

Shown in game with `!rules` and on the website. Written for players; Claude's draft, Jay can change any of it.

## The rules
- Be decent in public chat: no slurs, no threats, no spam. A filter blocks the worst words in English, Portuguese and Spanish.
- Player killing is part of the game where the zones allow it. Hunting newcomers again and again is not: the skull and bounty rules punish it.
- Only the cavebot built into the Spirebound client is allowed. Third-party bots and macros are cheating.
- Report bugs with `!report`; never use them for gain.
- No selling characters, items or gold for real money.
- Report a player: `!report <name>, <what happened>`. A gamemaster reads every report.

## What happens to rule breakers
1. A warning from a gamemaster.
2. A mute: 1 hour, then 24 hours, then 7 days.
3. A ban of the character: 1 day, then 7 days.
4. A ban of the account: 30 days.
5. A permanent ban. Cheating with third-party bots, selling accounts or gold for money, or abusing a bug for gain skips straight to step 3 or 5.

## For gamemasters
- `/reports` lists open reports (`/reports clear` when done). `/mute <name>, <minutes>` and `/unmute <name>`.
- `/gmlog` shows the last GM actions. Every mute, restore, calendar and scope change is written there and in the server log.
- Unfair Death Game deaths (a server crash or server lag): `/restore <name>`.
