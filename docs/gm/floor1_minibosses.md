# Floor 1 hidden mini-bosses (GM only)

Do not copy this into player docs, the website or the in-game guide. Players find these by exploring, and can sell each spot to Vesper as a secret.

Each boss wakes when someone steps on its secret's tile and it is not resting. After it dies it rests for a random 1 to 3 hours. Everyone who hurt it gets its trophy once per character. Rules: `server/data-spirebound/lib/spirebound/trophies.lua`.

| Boss | Where (secret) | Jump there | Good for | Trophy |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Old Tusk | Boar Meadow's Wallows (z 8), the dead-end mud chamber (s2) | `/pos 1159, 1369, 8` | level 15 solo, or a pair around 12 | +3% maximum health |
| Hollowfang | An abandoned bedroll deep in Wolf Copse (s9) | `/pos 1044, 1297, 7` | level 18 solo, or a pair around 14 | +5 movement speed |
| Briar Mother | Inside the bramble ring of Thornsnap Hollow (s11) | `/pos 1165, 1166, 7` | level 20 solo, or a pair around 16 | +2% damage |
| Drowned Warden | The ruined well in the far south-east corner (s10) | `/pos 1380, 1380, 7` | level 28 solo, or a group of 3 around 22 | one style level at once |

To test one: jump there with the GM character, step off and back onto the tile with a normal test character.
