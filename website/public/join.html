Join the tower

Opens with the server. Account sign-up opens here when the server opens. During the friends test, Jay sends you the address.

How joining works

  1. Read the rules. The sign-up page asks you to agree to them.
  2. Make an account. Your password is never stored as plain text. Extra accounts are fine (up to 3 game clients on one computer at once).
  3. Name your first character on the same page: 3 to 20 letters, each word starting with a capital (for example Mira or Tom Reed). Pick a name of your own: names from other games, real people's names and rude names break the rules, and a gamemaster will ask you to change them.
  4. Download the Spirebound client and log in with your e-mail and password.
  5. Pick your vocation in the game, not here. Every character wakes in the Arrival Yard with no vocation. A short guided path (under ten minutes) ends at the Registrar in Hearthgate, where you choose your vocation and your difficulty. You will know how fighting feels before you choose.

More characters

Each account can hold several characters. Making another one from this site comes with the full account pages; until then, ask a gamemaster in game.

Keeping your account safe

  • Use a password you use nowhere else.
  • Staff will never ask for your password, in game or anywhere else.
  • Too many wrong passwords from one address make that address wait a few minutes.
