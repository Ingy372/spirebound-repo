Welcome to the tower

An Open Tibia server shaped like a floating tower. Everyone starts on Floor 1. Each floor ends in a labyrinth and a boss. The first time anyone beats a floor boss, the next floor opens for the whole server.

What makes it different
  • One shared tower. Floors open for everyone, with a server-wide announcement.
  • PvP with teeth, not bullying. Newbie protection, level-range protection, outnumbered shields, bounties and harsh red-skull penalties.
  • Exploration pays. Hidden secrets across every floor, bought by the Info Broker.
  • Houses do things. Portals, Fortune Halls, trophy halls, training rooms, ponds.
  • Never pay-to-win. The best gear and spells come only from playing.
  • Choose your risk. Normal, Hardcore, or Death Game, where dying retires the character.

New here? Start with How to play. Pick your vocation on the Vocations page and your risk level on Difficulty.

Seasons: each season the tower resets with remixed bosses and secrets. Titles and cosmetics you earn stay on your account forever.

Spirebound is a fan-made, non-commercial Open Tibia project. Never pay-to-win. Building in public; this site is rebuilt from the game's own data files.
