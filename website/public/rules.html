Rules

Be decent

  • No harassment, hate speech or targeted abuse. PvP is fine; stalking someone out of the game is not.
  • No scams. Trades are your own risk; staff can see market and trade logs.
  • One person, up to two clients at once.

Bots and helpers

  • Our own client is the only allowed client. Its built-in cavebot is allowed: while it runs you earn 45% experience and half the loot, and the cut lasts 10 minutes after you switch it off. Manual play is more than twice as good.
  • Secrets, rare chests and the Twin Fang trial never trigger while the cavebot is on. Boss rooms do not let a cavebot character in.
  • Auto-targeting on its own costs nothing.
  • Third-party bots, modified clients, auto-fishing and hiding the cavebot are not allowed. First offense: 7-day ban and gains removed. Second: permanent.
  • Suspicious patterns are logged for staff review; no automatic bans.

Never pay-to-win

The donation shop sells cosmetics, housing decorations and conveniences only. Nothing in it gives damage, defense, healing, experience, skill speed, loot or gear stats. No experience boosts, no loot boxes, and shop items can never be gambled. Shop items can be traded on the market for gold, so players can earn cosmetics by playing.

Fair play for the Death Game

Only monsters, bosses, traps and the environment can retire a Death Game character. See Difficulty.

Spirebound is a fan-made, non-commercial Open Tibia project. Never pay-to-win. Building in public; this site is rebuilt from the game's own data files.
