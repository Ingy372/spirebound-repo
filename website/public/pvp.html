PvP rules

PvP is real, but the game protects newer and weaker players. Toxicity is the thing we are built against.

Where you can fight

ZoneRule
Safe (towns)No attacks of any kind: streets, temple, depot, bank, shops, houses.
GuardedRoads and starter fields. An unjust kill counts as three, and town guards hunt the killer for 30 minutes.
OpenMost hunting grounds. Normal skull rules.
ContestedEach floor's apex spawn. Kills give no skull; killers earn Blood Marks. Victims drop more.
DeadzoneThe deepest areas. Contested rules, bigger losses, the best experience and loot.

Protections

  1. Newbie protection: below level 50 you cannot attack players and they cannot attack you (except in boss rooms), and the border blocks you from Contested and Deadzone zones. New characters also get 2 hours of play-time protection.
  2. Level-range protection: you cannot start a fight with a player below 70% of your level, outside Contested, Deadzone and boss rooms. They can still defend themselves.
  3. Outnumbered shield: if 3 or more different players hit you within 10 seconds, you take 10% less player damage per attacker beyond 2 (up to 40% less).
  4. Guild-farming shield: if the same guild kills you twice within 6 hours outside a war, you get a 30-minute Sanctuary against that guild.
  5. Bounties on red players, paid only to killers who are not in the same guild, party or network as the target.
  6. Wars are by consent: both guild leaders must sign, and war kills never cause skulls.
  7. A kill on a player 30+ levels below you counts double toward skulls.
