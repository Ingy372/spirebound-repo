Difficulty

Chosen at creation. You can move down a difficulty at the Registrar, never up.

NormalHardcoreDeath Game

Exclusive-Hardcore titleDeathbound title, exclusive outfit, own leaderboard

Player killers can never delete a Death Game character. If another player damaged you in the 60 seconds before you died and did real damage (at least 15% of your health), or another player dragged the monster that killed you onto you, it counts as a PvP death: you are not deleted and take the Hardcore penalty instead. Party members, guildmates and same-IP characters do not count, so nobody can tag themselves immortal.

Unfair deaths. A Death Game character killed by a server crash or by lag on the server is restored by a gamemaster. Your own internet dropping is not unfair: the tower is cruel. Ask a gamemaster within a day.

Stepping down to Hardcore or Normal is allowed in town at any time, but it costs the extra experience the harder path gave you.

Spirebound is a fan-made, non-commercial Open Tibia project. Never pay-to-win. Building in public; this site is rebuilt from the game's own data files.
