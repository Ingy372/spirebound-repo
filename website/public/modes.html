Difficulty

Chosen at creation. You can move down a difficulty at the Registrar, never up.

NormalHardcoreDeath Game
DeathStandard Tibia penaltyHeavier penalty (1.5x skill and experience loss, double item drop chance)Character is retired to the Memorial
Experience bonusnone+25%+50%
Loot bonusnone+15%+30%
Exclusive-Hardcore titleDeathbound title, exclusive outfit, own leaderboard

Player killers can never delete a Death Game character. If another player damaged you in the 60 seconds before you died, it counts as a PvP death: you are not deleted and take the Hardcore penalty instead. Party members, guildmates and same-IP characters do not count, so nobody can tag themselves immortal.

Spirebound is a fan-made, non-commercial Open Tibia project. Never pay-to-win. Building in public; this site is rebuilt from the game's own data files.
