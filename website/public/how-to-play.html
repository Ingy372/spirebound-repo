How to play

Getting started

  1. Create an account on this site (opens with the server).
  2. Download the Spirebound client. It is the only allowed client; the cavebot and helpers are built in.
  3. Make a character and pick a vocation and difficulty. You can move down a difficulty later, never up.
  4. You start in Hearthgate. Talk to the people there: every quest says what to do next.

Sword skills

Melee and ranged attacks come mostly from sword skills on a hotbar (Shift+1 to Shift+9). Each weapon family has its own: Openers, Links and Finishers, chained for combos. Arcanists and Mystics also get big spells that unlock automatically as you level; no trainer, no gold.

Fishing, secrets and hunting tasks

Fish every floor for rare catches and a log to complete. Find hidden secrets and sell what you know to the Info Broker in Hearthgate. Take daily hunting tasks from Taskmaster Ren. There is no level cap.

Spirebound is a fan-made, non-commercial Open Tibia project. Never pay-to-win. Building in public; this site is rebuilt from the game's own data files.
