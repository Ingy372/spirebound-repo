Vocations

Four familiar vocations. Promotion at level 20 gives the familiar regeneration bump.

VocationLike Tibia'sWeaponsRole
VanguardKnightSword, Axe, Club (+ shield)Tank, front line
RangerPaladinBow, Crossbow, Spear (throwing)Ranged damage, some healing
ArcanistSorcererWandArea damage, big flashy spells
MysticDruidRodHealer, support, nature damage

Every vocation unlocks about 20 spells by level, including utility and support spells. The rare Twin Fang trial (Vanguards only, three per season) unlocks a second-blade fighting style.
