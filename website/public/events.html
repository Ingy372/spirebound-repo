Events

Times are server time.

EventWhenWhat
ArenaDaily, sign-ups at 19:00 and 22:00Last Man Standing, Team Rush and Capture the Banner on a walled arena. Same health and fixed hits for everyone, so level and gear do not matter. Nobody dies or loses anything. Level 20+, 4-20 fighters. Say !arena join.
Spire SiegeEvery Sunday, sign-ups 19:45, starts 20:00Guilds fight over a throne room for 30 minutes. The winner holds the keep for a week: +5% experience and loot and a leader title. Level-scaled like the Arena. Say !siege join.
Weekend bonusesFridays to SundaysRotating bonuses for fishing, secrets and experience.
New floorWhen a floor boss fallsThe whole server is told and the next floor opens for everyone.
SeasonsEvery few monthsThe tower resets and bosses and secrets are remixed. Titles and cosmetics are kept.
Spirebound is a fan-made, non-commercial Open Tibia project. Never pay-to-win. Building in public; this site is rebuilt from the game's own data files.
